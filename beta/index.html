Help us build Scout Fox Travel

We are looking for families to try Scout Fox Travel before it launches and tell us honestly what does and does not work.

This is pre-release software.

Features are incomplete, results may be wrong or missing, and things will break. That is expected — finding those problems is the point of the beta. Please do not rely on Scout Fox Travel to plan a trip you have already booked.

What we are asking of you

  • Try planning a real family trip using the tool.
  • Tell us where the suggestions felt wrong, irrelevant, or unsafe for your kids' ages.
  • Report anything that errors, hangs, or looks broken.
  • Expect roughly one short email from us every couple of weeks.
